How it works

Capture. Encrypt. Recall.

Three steps, and the middle one never leaves your device.

01

Capture

Save from the share sheet, the context menu, or a shortcut. Text, links, and images all land in one place.

02

Encrypt

Your device derives a key with PBKDF2 and seals each capture with AES-256-GCM before it touches disk.

03

Recall

Search locally, even offline. If you sync, only ciphertext leaves the device.

Private by design

The relay is dumb.
Your data isn’t.

Recall has no account system and no server-side search. Your passphrase is the only key, and it never leaves your device.

  • Passphrase only

    No email, no account, no recovery key. The passphrase you choose is the encryption secret.

  • AES-256-GCM

    PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations derives the key. Every capture gets a fresh 12-byte IV.

  • A dumb relay

    The relay stores ciphertext and minimal metadata. It cannot search, index, OCR, or decrypt.

  • Encrypted at rest

    SQLCipher on Android. An AES-GCM-wrapped SQLite file on Chrome and desktop. FTS5 lives inside.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

The important details before you trust Recall with your notes.

Read the docs
Do my devices need to be paired?

No. The sync identity is derived from your passphrase, so any device with the same passphrase joins the same encrypted stream. Use 4–6 diceware words — the passphrase is the security boundary.

What if I forget my passphrase?

There is no recovery flow. Recall cannot reset it or decrypt your captures without it. Keep the passphrase somewhere safe and separate from your devices.

How do I install the Chrome extension?

Download the ZIP and unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder. Edge works the same way. Recall is not in the Chrome Web Store.

Android says it can’t install the APK.

Allow installs from the app you downloaded it with — Files, Chrome, or your browser — in Android settings, then open the APK again. Recall requires Android 8.0 or newer.

Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

The build is not signed with a commercial Windows certificate. Choose More info → Run anyway, or verify the download against SHA256SUMS.txt first.
